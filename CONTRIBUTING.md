# Contributing

Contributions are welcome only when the contributor has the legal right to submit them and agrees to the repository’s contribution terms.

Before submitting a pull request, read CLA.md. In the pull-request description, include the statement:

> I have read and agree to the repository CLA.

By making that statement and submitting the contribution, you accept CLA.md for that contribution. Do not submit third-party code, copied material, model output, datasets, or other content unless you have the right to do so and you clearly disclose any license or attribution obligations.

A contribution may be rejected when its provenance, licensing, or ownership cannot be verified. Acceptance of a contribution does not grant the contributor commercial rights in the repository as a whole and does not change the repository license.

Security-sensitive issues should not include secrets, credentials, private keys, tokens, or confidential third-party information.
